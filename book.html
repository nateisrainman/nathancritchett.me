The Premise

The system you build for AI is the system that builds your people.

Every organization is racing to adopt AI. Most are racing in the wrong direction, optimizing for outputs and ignoring the humans who have to evaluate, apply, and lead with what AI produces.

Cognitive Architecture draws on the Model of Hierarchical Complexity, developmental psychology, complexity science, and lessons from biology and physics to explain how human thinking actually grows, and how to grow it on purpose in an AI-saturated world.

This is not a book about prompts. It is a book about the architecture of thought itself, and how to elevate it in yourself and others, so people and AI think better together instead of people quietly getting worse.

19% Less accurate Harvard found that elite consultants relying on AI were 19 percentage points less accurate on tasks at the edge of its capabilities. This book explains why, and what to do about it.

Build your own intelligence in Lane 1, then direct the machine's intelligence in Lane 2. The metacognitive practice that separates the Architect from the user.

About the Author

Nathan Critchett

Nathan Critchett is the founder of Arq.Training, a cognitive development platform that measures how complexly a person can think and builds the capacity to think at higher levels over time. In the early days of public access to generative AI, he built one of the first AI-in-education courses and trained capacity and tool use in person across public and private sectors.

He previously co-founded Edapt, and has delivered keynotes and trainings to a combined audience of more than 10,000 educators through partnerships with SSDA, ACSA, AASA, NREA, and WVASA. A lifelong learner and athlete, a former Division I baseball player and aspiring triathlete, he writes at the intersection of developmental science, artificial intelligence, and human performance.
