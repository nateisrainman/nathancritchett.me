Cognitive Architecture

How to think when machines think for you.

A synthesis of the thinkers and technologists shaping this moment, and a clear direction for the people who have to lead through it.

What the book covers:

  1. The core shift. Stop competing with the machine. Start directing it, and build the judgment to do it well.
  2. A direction for education. How schools help students think with AI instead of letting it think for them.
  3. A direction for the workforce. Where durable human value lives as the tools get more capable.
  4. Hiring for adaptability. Why the Adaptability Quotient beats the resume, and what Arq.Training is building to measure it.
  5. Lessons from the mound. What elite athletic development and arm health reveal about growing under pressure.
